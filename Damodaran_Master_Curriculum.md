## 1. Introduction & Learning Framework

Professor **Aswath Damodaran** (Stern School of Business at New York University) is widely regarded as the "Dean of Valuation." His website contains an immense collection of lectures, spreadsheets, datasets, papers, and books. However, because it has grown organically over 25+ years, it can feel overwhelming for a student to navigate without structure.

This document synthesizes **everything** on Damodaran Online into a single, cohesive, **chronological curriculum**. All key topics, video playlists, slides, reading materials, spreadsheet models, datasets, and assessments are organized in the exact order you should consume them.

### Phase 0: Mathematical & Accounting Foundations
Before diving into corporate finance and valuation, build the essential baseline in financial statements, applied statistics, and time value of money.

#### Topic 0.1: Minimalist Accounting for Finance
- **Goal**: Understand financial statements (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement) through the pragmatic lens of a finance professional rather than an accounting auditor.
- 📺 **Videos**: [Accounting Online Course Page](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastacctg.htm) | [YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUkh9m2BorqmKaLrNBjKtFDhpdFdi8f7C)
- 📖 **Readings**: [From Disclosures to Value Guide](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/eqnotes/PG/Reading10KPG.pdf)

#### Topic 0.2: Applied Statistics for Finance & Investing
- **Goal**: Learn data visualization, summary statistics, distributions, regressions, and correlation—specifically tailored to financial data.
- 📺 **Videos**: [Statistics Online Course Page](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcaststatistics.htm) | [YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUkh9m2BorqmXcRzWFbzcjMd7fYErVexF)

#### Topic 0.3: Foundations of Finance (Primer)
- **Goal**: Master Time Value of Money (TVM), interest rates, risk vs. return fundamentals, and basic valuation of bonds, stocks, and options (9 sessions).
- 📺 **Videos**: [Foundations Online Course Page](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastfoundationsonline.htm) | [YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUkh9m2BorqndWimijiJ-VCAXjJUrzJQU)

### Phase 1: Core Corporate Finance
Corporate Finance is the study of how businesses should make financial decisions to maximize value. It spans investment choices, financing mix, and cash returns.

#### Step 1.1: The Big Picture & First Principles
- **Topics**: Core objective function (maximize firm value), corporate governance, manager vs. stockholder conflicts.

### Phase 2: Core Valuation Principles & Intrinsic DCF
Valuation is where corporate finance decisions manifest in business value.

#### Step 2.1: Valuation Architecture & Intrinsic DCF Basics
- **Topics**: Valuation philosophies, intrinsic value vs. relative value vs. option pricing, Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) framework, Dividend Discount Model (DDM), Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE), Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF).
- 📺 **Videos**: [MBA Valuation Semester Webcasts](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcasteqspr25.htm) (Sessions 1–4) | [YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUkh9m2BorqkgpNyRpP-NL3BS4yvFabXk)

#### Step 2.2: Estimating Cash Flows, Growth Rates, and Discount Rates
- **Topics**: Normalizing cash flows, estimating reinvestment (CapEx, Working Capital, R&D), growth origins (historical, analyst, fundamental growth rate), compounding growth, discount rates (WACC vs. Cost of Equity).
- 📺 **Videos**: Sessions 5–12 of MBA Valuation
- 📖 **Readings**: *Investment Valuation* Chapters 7–11; Papers: [The Origins of Growth](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/papers/growthorigins.pdf), [Country Risk Premiums](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5354459)

## 4. Study Tips & How to Track Progress

1. **Focus on Concepts First, Tools Second**: Never treat an Excel spreadsheet as a black box. Always verify formulas and ensure you understand the underlying economic rationale.
2. **Follow the Video + Slide + Reading Triad**: For each step, watch the video lecture, review the slide presentation, and read the corresponding book chapter or paper.
3. **Practice with Real Companies**: Pick one real company (e.g., Apple, Disney, or a local company in your market) and apply every concept step-by-step as you progress through Phase 1 and Phase 2.
4. **Utilize Annual Data Updates**: Incorporate Damodaran's latest industry averages and Equity Risk Premiums from the [Current Data Page](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/datacurrent.html) into your valuations.
