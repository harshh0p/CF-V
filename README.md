> **An interactive, step-by-step guidebook and live synchronized companion to Prof. Aswath Damodaran’s NYU Stern corporate finance and valuation curriculum.**

Transforming hundreds of disparate university web pages, syllabi, spreadsheet templates, and global industry datasets from [Damodaran Online](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/) into a single, cohesive, chronological learning portal.

---

## 🧭 The 7-Step Guidebook Roadmap

The curriculum is structured chronologically so anyone—from a complete beginner to an experienced financial analyst—can master valuation systematically:

| Step | Stage | Duration | Core Focus & Milestones |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Mindset & Philosophy** | Day 1 | The Corporate Life Cycle, Narrative vs. Numbers, Valuation Myths & First Principles |
| **02** | **Prerequisite Primers** | Week 1 | 3 Foundational Primers: Minimalist Accounting, Applied Statistics, Time Value of Money (TVM) |
| **03** | **Select Learning Track** | Formats | Choose your track: Full 15-Week MBA Semester, 3-Day Executive Intensive, or Webcast Series |
| **04** | **Corporate Finance Core** | Weeks 2–12 | **36 Video Sessions**: Investment Principle (ROIC/IRR), Financing Mix (Optimal Debt/WACC), Dividend Policy |
| **05** | **Intrinsic Valuation Core** | Weeks 13–20 | **25 Video Sessions**: DCF cash flow estimation, risk-free rates, ERP, Ginzu models, and relative multiples |
| **06** | **Specialized & Real Options** | Weeks 21–22 | Valuing Financial Services & Banks, Startups, Distressed Companies, and Real Options (Expand/Delay/Abandon) |
| **07** | **Applied Practice & Live Analysis**| Ongoing | Real-company valuation project briefs, weekly finance puzzles, timed MBA quizzes, and live model builds |

## 🏛️ Comprehensive Reference Hubs

In addition to the 7-Step Roadmap, the portal features 5 dedicated reference libraries:

### 🎓 1. Teaching & Online Classes (`#teaching`)
- **7 Complete Online Courses**: Accounting (12 sessions), Statistics (10 sessions), Foundations of Finance (9 sessions), Corporate Finance Online (36 sessions), Valuation Online (25 sessions), Investment Philosophies (15 sessions), Corporate Life Cycles (20 sessions).
- **University MBA Courses**: Spring 2025 MBA Valuation, Undergraduate Valuation, MBA Corporate Finance semester webcasts and syllabi.
- **Executive Programs**: TriUM Global Executive MBA & Executive Valuation Seminars.

### 📚 2. Writing & Publications Hub (`#books-papers`)
- **Complete Bookshelf (11 Books)**: *Applied Corporate Finance (4th Ed)*, *Investment Valuation (4th Ed)*, *Damodaran on Valuation (2nd Ed)*, *The Dark Side of Valuation (3rd Ed)*, *The Little Book of Valuation*, *Narrative and Numbers*, *Corporate Life Cycles*, *Investment Philosophies (3rd Ed)*, *Investment Fables*, *Strategic Risk Taking*, *Corporate Finance (2nd Ed)* with chapter links and author prefaces.
- **SSRN Research Papers**: Academic papers categorized by Equity Risk Premiums, ESG, Value Investing, Risk-free Rates, Betas, Leases, R&D Capitalization, and Country Risk.
- **Thought Pieces & Glossary**: Selected opinion columns for the *Financial Times*, *WSJ*, and *Forbes*, plus a complete financial terms dictionary.

### 💻 3. Valuation & Finance Tools (`#tools`)
- **Corporate Finance Models**: WACC Calculator (`wacccalc.xls`), Capital Budgeting (`capbudg.xls`), Optimal Debt Structure (`capstru.xlsx`), APV Model (`apv.xls`), Dividend Assessment (`dividends.xls`), Share Buybacks Analyzer (`buybacks.xls`).
- **Valuation & Ginzu Models**: Simple Ginzu (`fcffsimpleginzu.xlsx`), Master FCFF Ginzu (`fcffginzu.xlsx`), FCFE Ginzu (`fcfeginzu.xls`), Dividend Discount Ginzu (`divginzu.xls`), Multiples Models (`eqmult.xls`, `firmmult.xls`), High Growth DCF (`higrowth.xls`), Distress Valuation (`distress.xls`), Bank Equity Return Model (`eqexret.xls`).
- **Real Option Calculators**: Option to Expand (`expand.xls`), Option to Delay (`delay.xls`), Natural Resource Options (`naturalresource.xls`).
- **Mobile Calculators**: Damodaran’s uValue iOS app companion links.

### 📊 4. Datasets & Benchmarks (`#reference`)
- **About Data**: Detailed notes on timing (updated annually every January), data history (1996–Present), data vendors, and usage rules.
- **Data Breakdown Categories**: Variables, 90+ industry groupings, and regional aggregates (US: 5,994 firms, Europe: 6,559 firms, Emerging Markets: 27,360 firms, Japan: 3,965 firms, Global: 48,156 firms).
- **Current Data (Jan 2026)**: 9 interactive sub-filter categories:
  - ⚡ *Risk & Discount Rates*: Industry Betas, Total Betas, WACC, Annual Implied ERP (1960–2026), Monthly ERP (2008–2026), Sovereign Country Risk Premiums (150+ countries).
  - 📈 *Investment Returns*: Historical returns for US Stocks, T-Bonds, T-Bills, Gold, Real Estate (1928–2026), Sector ROIC, ROE, and EVA.
  - ⚖️ *Capital Structure*: Book and Market Debt Ratios, Interest Coverage, Synthetic Ratings.
  - 💰 *Dividend Policy*: Yields, Payout Ratios, FCFE Yields, Stock Buyback totals.
  - 💵 *Cash Flows*: CapEx, Net CapEx, Depreciation, R&D Expenses, Working Capital.
  - 🌱 *Growth & Reinvestment*: Reinvestment Rates, Fundamental Growth in EBIT.
  - 🔢 *Multiples*: P/E, PEG, Price/Book, EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA, EV/Invested Capital.
  - 🏛️ *Corporate Governance*: Institutional holdings, insider ownership, CEO pay.
  - 🎯 *Option Datasets*: Equity and firm volatility across sectors.
- **Archived Datasets**: Year-by-year archives from 1999 to 2025 for quantitative backtesting.

### 🔄 5. Latest Updates & Additional Content Hub (`#updates`)
- **Live Blog Feed**: Real-time posts from Prof. Damodaran’s *Musings on Markets* blog with dates, summaries, and reading links.
- **Discovered & Unmapped Materials ("Additionals Hub")**: Solves the problem of unclassified resources. Whenever Damodaran uploads new spreadsheets, case notes, or seminars that fall outside the 7 core steps, the sync engine catalogs them here with direct download links.
- **Dataset Logs**: Schedule and change logs for the 2026 and 2027 annual releases.

## ⚡ Automated Sync Engine & CI/CD Pipeline

To ensure the platform never falls out of date with NYU Stern, an automated crawler pipeline is integrated directly into the repository:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ Prof. Damodaran Online Portals  │
│ pages.stern.nyu.edu & Blogspot  │
└────────────────┬────────────────┘
                 │
                 ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ sync_damodaran.js (Node.js)     │ ◄── Triggered weekly via GitHub Actions (.github/workflows/sync.yml)
│ - Crawls 6 core directory pages │     or locally via: node sync_damodaran.js
│ - Parses live Blogspot JSON feed│
│ - Discovers new spreadsheets    │
└────────────────┬────────────────┘
                 │
                 ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ updates_feed.json               │ ──► Ingested by index.html & script.js in real time
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Running the Sync Locally
```powershell
# Run the sync engine (takes ~5 seconds)
node sync_damodaran.js
```

### Automated GitHub Actions Workflow
The workflow at [`.github/workflows/sync.yml`](.github/workflows/sync.yml) runs:
- **Weekly Schedule**: Every Monday at 00:00 UTC.
- **Manual Trigger**: Can be run anytime via the GitHub Actions UI (*Workflow Dispatch*).
- **Auto-Commit**: If new blog posts or unmapped spreadsheets are detected, it automatically commits and pushes the updated `updates_feed.json` back to `main`.

## 🚀 Deployment to GitHub Pages

Deploying this portal to GitHub Pages takes under 1 minute:

1. Push this repository to your GitHub account:
   ```bash
   git remote add origin https://github.com/<your-username>/<repo-name>.git
   git branch -M main
   git push -u origin main
   ```
2. In your GitHub repository:
   - Go to **Settings** > **Pages**.
   - Under **Build and deployment** > **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
   - Select branch **`main`** and folder **`/ (root)`**, then click **Save**.
3. Your live portal will be available at:
   ```
   https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/
   ```
