How to Follow This Guidebook (For Any Skill Level)

Whether you are a complete beginner or a finance professional refreshing your skills, follow this recommended path.

LEVEL 1: COMPLETE BEGINNER Start at Step 1 & Step 2

Begin with Mindset & Framework Setup (Step 1) and complete the 3 foundational primers in Accounting, Statistics, & TVM (Step 2) before taking on Corporate Finance.

LEVEL 2: STUDENT / PRACTITIONER Jump to Step 3 & Step 4

Select your learning track (MBA Semester Class vs Executive 3-Day vs Webcasts) in Step 3 and dive directly into Corporate Finance Core (Step 4).

LEVEL 3: EXPERIENCED ANALYST Fast-Track to Step 5, 6 & 7

Go straight to Intrinsic & Relative Valuation (Step 5), Specialized Firm & Real Options Models (Step 6), and Live Real-Time Valuations & Datasets (Step 7).
